# Privacy Policy for Quick Maths

**Quick Maths** ("the App") is committed to respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you use the App on mobile devices or Android Automotive OS.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

**Quick Maths is designed to process data locally on your device.**

* **Handwriting Data & Touch Input:** Strokes drawn on screen via Jetpack Ink and recognized via Google ML Kit Digital Ink Recognition are processed entirely on-device. Your handwriting, recognized math equations, and inputs are never transmitted, saved to an external cloud server, or viewed by us.
* **Game Progress & Local Settings:** High scores, game settings, and local preferences are stored locally on your device's internal storage. This data is not uploaded anywhere.
* **No Account Required:** You are not required to create an account, log in, or provide personal details (such as your name, email, or phone number) to use the App.

---

### 2. Third-Party Services & Libraries

The App utilizes Google ML Kit for on-device handwriting recognition.

* **Google ML Kit:** While processing occurs on-device, Google ML Kit may collect standard system metrics, performance logs, or diagnostic data in accordance with Google's APIs Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. For more details, refer to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

The App does not integrate with any advertising networks, third-party user analytics platforms, or external tracking services.

---

### 3. Data Storage and Retention

* All data generated during gameplay remains on your device.
* Uninstalling the App or clearing its application data through device settings will permanently erase all locally saved game progress and preferences.

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy

Quick Maths does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or local equivalent age threshold). Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, the App complies with children's privacy standards (including COPPA).

---

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected by revising the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy and publishing the new version on our repository or hosting page.
